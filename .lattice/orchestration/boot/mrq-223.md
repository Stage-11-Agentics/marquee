## Three defects were found in review of MRQ-217. Do not reintroduce any of them.

1. **`clearMirrorOutbox` was being called whenever config was absent**, from a global middleware
   that runs on every request — so any transient loss of a credential silently destroyed the
   pending change feed. It is now exported and called from **nowhere**. In your ticket, disconnect
   is its one legitimate caller. Absent config is an inert off state, permanently.
2. **Two independent token buckets** against one Airtable base — `drainMirrorOutbox` and
   `reconcileMirror` each built their own, so a mixed queue batch could run at 8 req/s against a
   5 req/s ceiling while every individual ≤4 req/s assertion passed. `processMirrorQueue` now
   threads one shared `MirrorTokenBucket`. **Your inbound payload pull spends the same budget —
   take the shared limiter.**
3. **`attempts` was incremented and never read**, so a poison row would be re-claimed forever and,
   at 100 of them, starve every healthy row behind it. `MAX_MIRROR_ATTEMPTS` is now 5. Rows at the
   cap stay pending with `status='failed'`, which is why your settings screen must show *stuck*
   separately from *queued*.

The pattern worth internalising: all three passed every test. They were composition errors, and
the only thing that caught them was someone reading the diff and asking what happens when a
precondition is briefly false.

- **Test titles take `AC-nnn · ` or `CONTRACT · ` and nothing else.** The regex is
  `scripts/checks/trace-ac-core.mjs:45`; the separator is a middle dot with whitespace either side.
  `MRQ-223 · ` is not in the vocabulary and `trace:ac` will exit 1 on every test you write. Your
