Struck **AC-239** carries a tombstone row, not a test. Ticket references are `MRQ-n` from [`ticket-map.md`](ticket-map.md).

**`runnable_at` has exactly two values.**

- **`pre-merge-static`** — provable by the PR's own CI with no deployed instance: `npm test` (hermetic — local D1 via `@cloudflare/vitest-pool-workers`, stubbed Resend and R2, no network, small deterministic fixture), `check:repo`, `trace:ac`, golden files, static and route scans.
- **`post-merge-smoke`** — needs the deployed, seeded instance or an outside judge: every `e2e:` run (EVALUATION §1.2 — e2e is the only suite permitted to assume a deployed instance, and it always runs against a real Worker on the real plan, never `wrangler dev`), plus `speed:`, `seed:`, `mirror:`, `api:` parity replay, `readme:`, the three oracle smokes, and every `felt` and `op-assist` criterion.

Where a criterion has a hermetic half and a deployed half, it gets **two rows** — the hermetic half must not wait for a deploy, and the deployed half must not be claimed by a unit test.

`felt` and `op-assist` criteria are smoke-side by construction. `oracle` rows name their oracle: a real Gmail, Outlook, or Apple Calendar inbox for the ICS chain; a real inbox for mail delivery; a clean headless agent for `check:skill-agent`. **Speed rows follow the client ruling of 2026-08-09:** AC-sourced budgets are pass/fail; the seven client-signed *objective* budgets are measured and reported with a ⚠ banner and never fail a run.
