# MRQ-212: Organizer governance: role legend, Owner-only removal, owner transfer, stale seats

Rulings 2026-08-14 (sequence/org-settings-design.md iteration 3; design contract prototypes/pipeline-v1.1 v1.15 #org/organizers). (1) Role legend as ruled — Owner / Organizer / Ops / Reviewer, one capability line each — rendered on the Organizers surface and live under the invite modal's role picker (the Reviewer line says sign-in only, assign work in Evaluation). UI 'Organizer' maps to schema program_lead; write the mapping down once where roles render. (2) Permission model: the org-settings surface is visible to Owner + org-wide Organizers only; only the Owner removes members; Organizer and up invite. Enforce server-side, reflect in UI (permission-aware action column exactly as the prototype demonstrates). (3) Owner transfer, safe and simple: Owner-only action on their own row; eligible recipients are org-wide Organizers who have signed in; confirm requires the organization name typed back; the flip is immediate and atomic, old owner becomes Organizer, both notified by mail (outbox rules), recorded to audit_log. (4) Stale seats: a seat scoped to an ended conference never expires silently — banner on the Organizers surface + data for the Org Home attention line. Builds directly on MRQ-207's removal-cascade and scoped-invite work — coordinate, do not duplicate.
