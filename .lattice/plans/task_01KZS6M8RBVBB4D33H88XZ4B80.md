# KYS-2: Admin shell shows no identity and has no sign-out

From KYS judge-path QA, local run 2026-08-11. After entering a demo persona there is no visible indication of who you are (the account menu route responds 'not installed') and no sign-out anywhere in the admin shell; the speaker portal has one, the organizer side does not. The server-side half of this trap is already dead (PR #7 replaces the session cleanly on re-auth, verified locally: speaker → organizer switch works on one cookie). What remains is orientation: a judge flipping between personas cannot tell which hat they wear or take it off. Suggest: current persona name + role in the shell header, and a sign-out entry next to it. Value: this is the first thing a rubric evaluator does when exploring both demos back to back.
