# MRQ-216: Demo identity does not round-trip: portal deep-link locks admin out until landing re-entry

Found during the mobile QA sweep (not a mobile issue — routed out of MRQ-215). Visiting /portal?event=…&person=per_… switches the demo identity to that speaker, after which every admin route returns 'Your account does not have access to this.' ?demo=organizer does NOT switch back — only re-entering from the landing page does. The denied surface itself is well built (names the reason, offers 'Open your speaker portal' / 'View the agenda'), so this is a round-trip gap rather than a dead end — but it will bite every agent and demo-driver who walks a portal deep-link then returns to admin. Acceptance: after a portal deep-link, ?demo=organizer (or an equivalent explicit switch) restores the organizer identity without a landing-page round trip.
