The first version of this fix passed its unit tests and 500'd the entire app on its first request. Found only by loading the app in a browser; nothing went red. Any future .catch()/.transform() on a schema that reaches the OpenAPI document must restate type/enum/minimum/maximum/default explicitly via .openapi().
