What moved, and why

30 rows → 22 · one ladder, not four · icons only where they mean something
  1. The Pipeline group is gone. The seven stages already live in three better places: the Program-home strip (clickable, with live counts — the sidebar rows never had counts), the Program board's columns, and the sessions list's status filter. The home screen is the spine; the nav stops photocopying it.
  2. “Modules” split into the organizer's language. Speaker ops (the chase work: Onboarding, Tasks, Communications, Files, Follow-ups), Call for proposals (Forms, Evaluation, Reviewer), Public (the three surfaces that leave the shell). “Modules” was software's word.
  3. Onboarding moved to Speaker ops. It was stage 5 of the ladder, but it is a place you work (the chase board), not a filter — it belongs with Tasks and Communications.
  4. “Add a session” is a + on the Sessions row, matching the existing ＋ beside the conference switcher. Creation is an action, not a destination.
  5. Icons curated. The geometry soup (◉ ◈ ▤ ▥ ◍ ◇ ◨ ◌ ◎ — with ▤ used three times) is gone. What stays carries meaning: trailing ↗ on the rows that leave the shell, ✉, ⚙, ⌘, ↻. The 16px icon column is reserved on every row, so labels stay aligned and nothing jumps.
  6. Labels shortened by their group label. “CFP forms” → Forms under Call for proposals; “Evaluation plan” → Evaluation; “Reviewer home” → Reviewer; “Speaker follow-ups” → Follow-ups under Speaker ops; “Conference settings” → Settings; “Sourcing pipeline” → Sourcing (the pipeline-vs-pipeline collision dies with the ladder).
  7. System health joins the demo chrome in the footer with API & CLI and Reset demo — ops utilities, not a one-row “System” group mid-nav.

Ruled (Atin, 2026-08-14)

R1 · “Abstracts & sessions” stays The long label wins — it teaches the abstract/session data-model distinction. “Program board” also keeps its full name. Reviewer stays in the organizer nav for now.
R2 · Lists folds into People A list is a named group of people — a saved view over the People table, not a separate place. It becomes a tab inside the People screen; Organization tightens to People · Sourcing. (Build change pending, with the rest.)
Status The interactive prototype (pipeline-v1.1, v1.13) now carries this sidebar — drive it there. Build untouched until the love pass.
