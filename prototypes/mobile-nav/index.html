The bottom strip retires. A drawer arrives.

At ≤760px the sidebar currently becomes a 54px bottom strip: fifteen destinations clipped to their first 1.35 characters, horizontally scrollable with no affordance, parked in the exact zone iOS Safari draws its own toolbar. It also hides the conference switcher, the group labels, and the footer — on a phone you cannot switch conferences at all. The proposal: a hamburger top-left in the topbar; tap → the real sidebar slides in as an overlay drawer, verbatim. Both frames below are live — drive them.

Tap ≡. Everything the desktop sidebar has, one tap away.
  • Full parity: switcher, group labels, all 21 destinations, footer — first time any of it is reachable on a phone.
  • Dismissal: scrim tap, ✕, Esc, or any navigation. Focus returns to ≡.
  • Nothing jumps: the drawer overlays; the page never shifts. Safari keeps its zone.

The ruling this encodes

  • ≤760px: drawer. The bottom strip is deleted, not restyled — the page gets its bottom padding back, Safari's toolbar owns that zone alone.
  • 761–1000px: the icon rail stays as shipped. Only the genuinely broken band changes (operator ruling, 2026-08-14).
  • The drawer is the Sidebar component, verbatim — same markup, CSS repositions it. One nav implementation; no second truth to drift. It scrolls internally, exactly as the desktop column does.
  • Touch geometry: rows are 40px minimum in the drawer (desktop keeps 34px); ≡ and ✕ are ≥34px boxes. The ≡ button exists only at ≤760px — no desktop layout change.
  • Motion: 160ms ease-out slide + scrim fade; none under prefers-reduced-motion. A panel, not an iOS sheet: hairlines and the scrim carry depth, no blur, no bounce.
  • While open: page scroll locks; drawer takes dialog semantics (aria-modal, focus to ✕ on open, back to ≡ on close).

  4. The stage flyout is already display:none below 1000px — the drawer never meets it.
  5. Scrollbar theming (operator, 2026-08-14): the drawer's internal scrollbar must follow the active theme — scrollbar-width: thin; scrollbar-color: var(--line-strong) transparent on .sidebar plus the ::-webkit-scrollbar pair, all token-driven so dark registers inherit without per-theme rules. Never leave the OS-default scrollbar inside themed chrome.

Open questions

Q2 · Theme switch — resolved by measurement. Mobile QA (M-01) measured the shipped topbar at 549px in a 373px viewport: .global-search gets width:100% at ≤760px and the 118px theme select rides beside it, dragging every screen into a 176px page-level horizontal scroll and pushing the account button off-viewport. The fold fixes both: search becomes flex:1; min-width:0, and the theme select moves into the drawer footer at phone width. Mobile topbar = ≡, search, avatar — nothing else.
